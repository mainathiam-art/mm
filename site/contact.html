Can I be a guest on the show?

Yes. Students, teachers, parents and neighbours have all been on. Pitch yourself in the form above — one sentence is enough to start.

Do I need experience to join the team?

None at all. We teach recording, editing, interviewing and research on the job. Come to a Thursday meeting and see if you like it.

Can I use your episodes for a class project?

Go ahead — just credit Between the Bells and the episode number. For anything bigger than a school project, email us first.
