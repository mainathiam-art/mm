Before you write

How to get on the show

We say yes more often than you'd think. A good pitch is one sentence long and a little bit uncomfortable.

  • 01

    Tell us what you'd want to talk about in one sentence. “Something about pressure” is a start; “why everyone lies about how much they study” is a pitch.

  • 02

    You don't need to be polished. We record for forty minutes and edit it down — the good bits are usually the ones you didn't plan.

  • 03

    If your story involves someone else, we'll ask them first. Nothing goes out without a yes.
